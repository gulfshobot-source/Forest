# Forest Sketchpad / Incubator

This repository is the experimental companion to the canonical Forest in `gulfshobot-source/the-forest`.

## Role

Use this repository for material that is valuable enough to preserve but not mature enough to become canonical.

This includes:

- raw AI-to-AI handoffs
- speculative architectures
- competing interpretations
- mathematical probes
- visual experiments
- prototype grammars and schemas
- contradictions and discarded approaches
- research notes
- implementation spikes
- provisional terminology
- unresolved conceptual fields

## Canonical Boundary

`gulfshobot-source/the-forest` remains the canonical structural Forest.

Nothing in this sketchpad becomes authoritative merely because it is persisted here.

Promotion path:

`raw signal / experiment -> sketchpad evidence -> validation / reconciliation -> deliberate canonical promotion`

## Operating Rules

1. Preserve source language before interpretation.
2. Label user-originated material separately from AI-generated interpretations.
3. Preserve contradictions and failed forms when they carry signal.
4. Prefer parallel hypotheses over premature convergence.
5. Do not silently promote sketchpad material into canonical state.
6. Every promotion candidate should state what survived testing, what changed, and what remains uncertain.
7. The sketchpad may be messy; provenance may not be.
8. Experiments should be designed to teach the Forest something, not merely produce artifacts.

## Relationship to the Canonical Forest

The sketchpad is a laboratory and nursery. The canonical Forest is the maintained organism.

A sketchpad artifact may eventually become:

- a canonical architecture rule
- a new tree, branch, vine, capability, object class, or visual grammar
- an implementation in the Control Surface, World Engine, Downstairs Connector Fabric, Agentic Harness, or another subsystem
- a rejected experiment retained as evidence

Until then it remains provisional.
